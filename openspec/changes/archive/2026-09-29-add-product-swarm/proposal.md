# Product swarm cockpit

## Why
One product spans repositories. Separate low-level sessions do not provide a
durable chief conversation, approval boundaries or differentiated model budgets.

## What changes
- Add product messages, feature specifications and server-computed approval digests.
- Route planning and implementation to explicit strong/cheap profiles.
- Load trusted BMAD resources and cap global model subprocess concurrency at five.
- Add an opt-in product cockpit, preserving legacy sessions and Gantt.
- Gate publication on allowlisted destinations, verification and exact-SHA CI.

## Impact
Additive PostgreSQL migrations; opt-in WHILLY_PRODUCT_SWARM=1. No merge or deploy.
Private registries and credentials remain outside this public repository.
