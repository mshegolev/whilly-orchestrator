## Why
Operators need keyboard access to visible controls while preserving terminal-style appearance and avoiding accidental task execution.

## What Changes
- F shows focus-only labels; ? opens help; Escape closes either mode.
- Native Tab and activation remain unchanged; typing, modifiers and composition are not intercepted.
- The common controller survives dashboard partial refresh.

## Impact
- web-status-ui; three operator pages and shared CSS/JavaScript.
